Marketing & Communications Leader | Financial services

A curious marketer, fluent in finance and agentic AI (and French)

I’m a B2B marketing and communications leader with more than a decade in asset management, across all asset classes in the public and private markets. I’m at home with the complexity of financial services, and I enjoy taking content that could easily be dry and making people want to read it.

It all starts with strategic planning, turning business goals into insight-led strategy with executive and sales teams.

From there come the global integrated campaigns, the brand positioning, the narrative, and a rich mix of content - including fund guides, RFP responses, press releases, social media posts, web copy, and even newsletters on luxury watches or the surprising afterlife of tennis balls.

Along the way, I’ve also led in-house and agency teams, run cross-functional projects and events, managed budgets, and set the KPIs and reporting that show what’s working (and what isn’t).

More recently, curiosity got the better of me. I started building agentic AI systems that take the repetitive tasks out of marketing, so teams have more time for the thinking and the ideas.

Outside work, I’m a charity Trustee. Sitting on the other side of the boardroom table has sharpened my strategic oversight, governance acumen and senior stakeholder engagement skills.

I’m now looking for my next marketing and communications leadership role in financial services. If you’re hiring, or simply fancy a chat, I’d love to hear from you.
